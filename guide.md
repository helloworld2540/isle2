# Isle 2 — How to Play

Welcome to Isle 2! This is your guide to the minigames, the shop, and how everything connects.

---

## The Basics

Isle 2 is split into a few areas you reach from the **world map**:
- **Upcycle** — a sorting minigame
- **Reef Rescue** — a collection minigame
- **Shop / Trader** — where you sell trash for score
- **Stats, Settings, and Tutorial** screens

Everything you collect feeds one shared economy. Score, trash, and upgrades persist between games, so nothing is lost when you switch areas.

---

## How the Economy Works

You collect five kinds of trash:
- **Polyethylene**
- **Polypropylene**
- **Glass**
- **Nylon**
- **Reef Debris**

You sell them at the Shop for **score** (your main currency). Score is used to buy upgrades like Deep Cache and inventory capacity.

Each material has a different value:
- Glass — 5 per unit *(best value)*
- Nylon — 4 per unit
- Polypropylene — 3 per unit
- Polyethylene — 2 per unit
- Reef Debris — 1 per unit *(cheapest, but plentiful)*

**Tip:** Sell pricier materials first, and try to sell **5 or more of one type at once** for a +25% bulk bonus!

### Your Inventory
You start with room for **30 units** of trash. You can upgrade this to hold more. If your inventory is full, you can't collect more trash until you sell some or upgrade capacity.

## Minigame 1: Upcycle (Sorting)

**Goal:** Sort trash on a conveyor belt into the right material bins.

### How to play
- Items scroll down a belt, each made of one of four materials (Polyethylene, Glass, Polypropylene, Nylon).
- Drag/swipe items to slide them into the matching bin.
- Correct sorting builds your **sort streak** — consecutive good sorts make it grow.
- Watch out for the **Fire** hazard zone.
- You can pick up and carry one item at a time, and drop it into a bin.

### Scoring
- Every good sort raises your **best sort streak** record.
- Your overall best upcycle score is tracked and shown on the main menu.

### Leaving
- Press **ESC** or click **Return to Map** to leave.

---

## Minigame 2: Reef Rescue (Collection)

**Goal:** Dive with a drone and collect garbage while avoiding fish.

### How to play
- Drag the screen (mouse or touch) to move your drone — it has momentum and inertia, so plan ahead.
- Garbage drifts by; touch it to collect.
- **Fish bump into you** and each counts as one "life lost." Three fish hits and the run ends.
- Off-screen items show small arrow markers so you know where to look.

### Combos
Collect **3 garbage items within 2 seconds** to trigger a combo multiplier (×2, ×3, and higher), giving you bonus points and bigger catch rewards.

### Depth
The deeper you go ( descend by moving down ), the faster things move. Your depth is shown and your **best depth** is recorded.

### End of run rewards
When the run ends (by fish hits or by you leaving), your collected garbage is converted into **Reef Debris** in your inventory. The deeper you collected, the more debris you earn.

- With the **Deep Cache** upgrade, you earn even more debris per run.

### Leaving
- Press **ESC** or click **Return to Map** to finish early and keep your rewards.

---

## The Shop & Trader

This is where you turn trash into score.

### Selling
- **Sell 1** — sells one unit of a material for its rate in score.
- **Sell All** — sells everything of that material at once. Sell **5+ units** to get a **+25% bulk bonus**.
- **Undo last sale** — reverts your most recent sale if you change your mind (capacity permitting).

### Read the HUD
You'll see:
- Your **score**.
- Your **inventory** counts per material.
- **Potential earnings** if you sold everything right now.
- **Tips** (inventory full / heavy / sort efficiently).

---

## Upgrades

### Inventory Capacity
- Starts at 30 units.
- Each level costs trash (8 + 4×current level) and adds **+15 units** capacity.

### Deep Cache (Reef Rescue boost)
- **Purchase:** 30 score.
- **Upgrade:** 15 × tier score.
- Each tier increases the debris bonus from Reef Rescue runs.

### Movement & Collection (world upgrades)
- Move speed: **+8%** per level.
- Collection radius: **+12 px** per level.
- (Cost/leveling handled in the UI.)

---

## Tips & Strategy

- **Sell bulk** — aim for 5+ of a material to trigger the +25% bonus.
- **Focus on high-value materials** first: Glass and Nylon beat everything else.
- Reef Debris is cheap but you get a lot from Reef Rescue — use the shop's **Sell All** to clear it quickly.
- **Buy Deep Cache early** to multiply your Reef Rescue debris earnings.
- Keep inventory under 80% to avoid the "heavy" warning and capacity blocks.
- In Reef Rescue, stay calm — combo windows are 2 seconds; prioritize collection over chasing fish.
- Don't bump fish! Three hits and you're done; leaving early is sometimes smarter.

## The World Map & Portals

You move around the island on foot. Portals (glowing pads) lead to the minigames and screens. Walk over a pad to trigger it.

- **Upcycle portal** — enters the sorting minigame.
- **Reef Rescue portal** — enters the ocean dive minigame.
- **Shop / Trader portal** — goes to the shop.
- **Stats, Settings, Tutorial** portals — open their screens.

If you try to enter a minigame portal with no trash collected yet, you may see a hint that you should try Reef Rescue first.

## NPCs (World Characters)

Friendly characters roam the island. Walk up to one to talk.

### Your first visit
- You're greeted with a welcome message.
- You **receive a starter reward** of trash (5 Polyethylene) the first time you meet them.

### Visits afterwards
- They offer **quests**: bring them a certain amount of a material in exchange for a reward (trash of another type).
- Quests have **two tiers**: complete tier 1 to unlock tier 2, which asks for a different material.
- Each NPC shows their quest progress above their head: `Quest: X/Y <material>`.
- When you have enough, click **Turn in** during the chat to receive the reward.
- Quest-ready NPCs **glow golden** so you can spot them easily.
- After completing both quests, they celebrate and say you're done.

### NPC name labels
- A `*` after the name means tier 1 quest done; `**` means both done.
- `(visited)` means you've talked before but no quest done yet.

### Daily flavor
Each NPC also drops a random flavor line so the island feels lively.

---



- Your progress (score, trash, best runs, upgrades, volumes) is **auto-saved** after every action.
- From the main menu, **New Save** wipes everything; **Load Save** continues.
- Save files are stored locally on your device.

Enjoy cleaning up the Isle!
