GOAL

SHIPPED

[ITERATION 12] 2026-11-16 - Economy ledger: exact-change spending, clear labels
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM (found by auditing the shared economy rather than a report)
  GameState.spend_trash() used a greedy walk in dictionary order: it took as
  many units as possible from Polyethylene, then Polypropylene, and so on. A
  basket of 1x Polyethylene (1 kg) + 2x Polypropylene (2 kg) could not pay a
  4 kg upgrade even though 2+2 is exact, because the 1 kg bottle was spent
  first and the remaining 3 kg could not be matched - the purchase silently
  failed with no feedback.

SHIPPED
  * spend_trash() now sorts materials heaviest-first and, if the greedy pick
    cannot match the amount, falls back to a bounded backtracking search
    (_plan_spend over the five material types). Nothing is deducted unless an
    exact plan is found.
  * GameState.TRASH_SHORT table (PET / PP / GL / NY / Debris) so short labels
    are defined once instead of substr() guesses.
  * Upcycle HUD uses it: "Recycled 5 | PET:0 GL:3 PP:2 NY:0" instead of the old
    ambiguous "Poly:0 ... Poly:0" where Polyethylene and Polypropylene shared
    the same abbreviation.

VERIFIED (live, editor bridge)
  * 1x PET + 2x PP, cost 4: succeeds, PP 2 -> 0, PET kept (the exact case the
    old greedy implementation could not pay).
  * 1x PET (1 kg) only, cost 3: returns false and leaves the inventory
    untouched.
  * 1x PET + 1x PP, cost 3: exact match, bag ends at 0 kg.
  * Upcycle HUD renders distinct PET/PP/GL/NY abbreviations.
  * Headless boot of all six scenes: no script errors.

BACKLOG (updated)
  - Consider a directional flow on the loop trail once a shader budget exists.
  - AudioManager could batch volume writes (single save for a multi-slider
    change).
  - Shop undo restores the exact units sold but not the bulk bonus tag; fine
    for now, worth a test when sale rules change again.
